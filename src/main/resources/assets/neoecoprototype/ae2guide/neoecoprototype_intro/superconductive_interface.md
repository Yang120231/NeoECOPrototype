# Energized Superconductive Interface

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="neoecoprototype:superconductive_interface" scale="3"></BlockImage>
</Row>

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="neoecoprototype:cable_superconductive_interface" />
</ItemGrid>

The higher tier of the two interfaces: the same doubled layout as the [L1 Powered ME Interface](powered_interface.md) - 18 config slots, 18 stock slots - but **each config slot** holds **8,192** items, or **512,000 mB** of fluid or chemical, and it generates **4,000 AE/t**. Partitioning and setting it up is the same as any ME Interface.

It idles at **0 AE/t**, and 4,000 AE/t is usually higher than every other passive generator in the network, so one of these takes over its passive generation.

The block is assembled in eco's <ItemLink id="neoecoae:integrated_working_station" /> from 1 Cryotheum Crystal, 4 [L1 Powered ME Interfaces](powered_interface.md), 9 Energized Superconductive Ingots and 4 Superconducting Processors, for 10,000 FE.
